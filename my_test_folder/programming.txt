Programming is the art of telling another human what you want the computer to do. The most important skills for a programmer are problem-solving, debugging, and the ability to learn new technologies. Modern programming languages like Rust, Python, and Go have different paradigms and strengths. Rust is known for its memory safety and performance, making it ideal for systems programming. Python excels in data science and machine learning. Learning multiple languages broadens your perspective and makes you a better developer.
